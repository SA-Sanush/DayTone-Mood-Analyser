The Interactive 3D Mood Orb

Biometric feedback & real-time shader dynamics

The 3D Orb on your dashboard acts as an intuitive biofeedback mirror of your emotional telemetry:

Colors (Mood Score)

Smoothly transitions across circadian HSL palettes. High scores (4–5/5) radiate tranquil cerulean & jade hues, while lower scores shift to cautionary warmth.

Deformation (Stress)

Orb vertices mutate via simplex noise algorithms. Elevated tension triggers faster turbulence ripples, providing immediate somatic awareness.

Calm Mode

For motion sensitivity, enable Calm Mode in Settings to freeze 3D canvas rendering and reduce transitions.

Understanding Wellness Goals

Rolling metrics & adaptive progress calculation

DayTone calculates goal progress using an adaptive rolling average window rather than rigid calendar weeks:

Rolling 7-Day Window

When you target wellness habits (e.g., 8 hours of sleep or mindful walks), DayTone evaluates check-ins over the past 7 days to absorb natural daily fluctuations without breaking your momentum.

Instant Historical Baseline

Newly created goals automatically integrate your existing check-in logs from the preceding 7 days, ensuring you don't start at an artificial 0% deficit on Day One.

AI Burnout Predictions & Transparency

Audited machine learning models & user data autonomy

DayTone uses a supervised machine learning classifier (Decision Tree / Random Forest) trained on standard occupational wellness metrics to classify burnout risk (Low, Medium, High).

Privacy & Ethical Opt-Out

If you prefer not to have ML models evaluate your entries, you can disable AI classifications anytime under your Settings Profile. Your data remains fully encrypted at rest, and risk metrics default to Low.
