Learn how DayTone tracks mood, computes goals, and classifies burnout risk.

The Interactive 3D Mood Orb

The 3D Orb on your dashboard is a dynamic visualization of your current wellness state:

  • Colors (Mood Score): Smoothly transitions between HSL states. A high score (e.g. 5/5) shows cool, calming shades, while lower scores show vibrant warning shades.
  • Shape Deformation (Stress Level): The orb deforms using simplex noise algorithms. High stress levels trigger stronger, faster wave deformations, providing immediate visual feedback of tension levels.
  • Calm Mode: If you are sensitive to movement, you can enable Calm Mode in your Settings to freeze all 3D canvas rendering and chart transitions.

Understanding Wellness Goals

DayTone calculates goals progress using a rolling average window:

  • When you create a goal (e.g., target 8 hours of sleep), DayTone evaluates your check-ins over a rolling 7-day window.
  • For new goals, DayTone immediately integrates historical logs from the past week so you don't start at 0% progress on Day One.

AI Burnout Predictions & Transparency

DayTone uses a supervised machine learning classifier (Decision Tree / Random Forest) trained on standard occupational wellness metrics to predict burnout risk (Low, Medium, High).

Privacy & Ethical Opt-Out: If you do not wish to have predictions run on your data, you can disable AI classifications anytime under your Settings profile. Your data will remain encrypted at rest, and risk metrics will default to Low.

For a complete technical analysis of model accuracy, feature weights, bias auditing, and performance indicators, please read the official Model Card Reference.
