Privacy Policy

Effective date: June 2026 — Last updated: June 2026

DayTone is a personal wellness tracking tool built for academic demonstration and individual use. We take your privacy seriously. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, and what rights you have.

1. What data we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address (required to create an account).
  • Profile information: optional age, gender, and occupation you provide during registration.
  • Daily mood logs: mood score, sleep hours, stress level, social interaction, physical activity, and an optional journal note (all self-reported).
  • Sentiment score: a numeric sentiment value derived locally from your journal note using VADER — no text is transmitted externally.
  • Burnout predictions: the AI-generated Low / Medium / High risk classification and associated confidence score.

2. How your data is stored

  • All data is stored in a local database (SQLite for development, PostgreSQL for production deployments).
  • Passwords are hashed using scrypt — we never store plaintext passwords.
  • Journal notes are encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption when the ENCRYPTION_KEY environment variable is configured.
  • Sessions are secured with HTTP-only cookies and CSRF protection on all state-changing forms.

3. How we use your data

  • To provide your personal dashboard, charts, and burnout risk predictions.
  • To generate personalised wellness suggestions based on your log history.
  • To send optional daily check-in reminder emails — only if you opt in during registration or in Settings.
  • To allow administrators to monitor platform health and assist with technical issues.

4. What we do not do

  • We never sell your personal data to third parties.
  • We do not share your individual data with advertisers, research firms, or any external organisation.
  • We do not use your data to train external AI models.
  • We do not process special-category health data — DayTone is a self-tracking tool, not a medical service.

5. Your rights

  • Access & portability: Export all your data as a JSON file from Settings → Export data.
  • Rectification: Edit any mood log from the History page, or update your profile in Settings.
  • Erasure (Right to be Forgotten): Delete your account and all associated data permanently from Settings → Delete account. This action is irreversible.

6. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all personal data (mood logs, notes, goals, burnout history, and profile) is permanently removed from the database. Backup files, if any exist, are overwritten on the next scheduled cycle.

7. Not a medical service

DayTone is an informational wellness tool. AI-generated burnout risk predictions are not medical diagnoses. They should not replace professional mental health support. If you are in distress, please reach out to a qualified professional or a crisis helpline.

8. Contact

Questions about this policy? Email us at privacy@daytone.example.
