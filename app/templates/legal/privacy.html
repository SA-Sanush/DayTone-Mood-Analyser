Introduction

DayTone is a personal wellness tracking sanctuary built for individual reflection and academic demonstration. We treat your personal telemetry with strict confidentiality. This policy outlines our data collection, cryptographic storage, usage boundaries, and user autonomy mechanisms.

1. What Data We Collect

  • Account Information: Your display name and email address, required to register and authenticate.
  • Profile Data: Optional demographic attributes (age, gender, and occupation) provided voluntarily during onboarding or in Settings.
  • Daily Mood Logs: Self-reported ratings for mood score, sleep duration, stress levels, social interaction, physical activity, and optional personal journal notes.
  • Sentiment Score: A local mathematical polarity score calculated locally from journal text using VADER — no text is ever transmitted to external NLP APIs.
  • Burnout Projections: The AI-computed risk tier (Low, Medium, or High) and associated model confidence scores.

2. Storage & Cryptographic Standards

  • Database Storage: Telemetry is held within isolated database tables with foreign-key referential integrity.
  • Password Hashing: Credentials are encrypted with salted scrypt hashes — plaintext passwords never touch disk.
  • Encrypted Notes: Journal entries are encrypted at rest using Fernet symmetric encryption when the application key is configured.
  • Secure Sessions: Authentication sessions use HTTP-only, SameSite cookies with mandatory CSRF token validation on all state-altering endpoints.

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To display circadian trends, interactive charts, and real-time 3D mood orb shader dynamics.
  • To calculate rolling average goal completions and historical progress baselines.
  • To generate client-side wellness reflections and burnout prevention suggestions.
  • To send optional daily check-in reminders — strictly if you explicitly toggle reminders on.
  • To support platform health diagnostics and error auditing by verified administrators.

4. Prohibitions & Data Protection Guarantees

  • We never sell, lease, or monetize your personal telemetry with third parties.
  • We do not broker data with advertising networks or commercial brokers.
  • We never feed your private journals into public machine learning models.
  • We do not process clinical medical records — DayTone remains a personal reflection companion.

5. User Autonomy & Data Rights

  • Right of Access & Portability: Export your full personal telemetry as a standardized JSON package anytime under Settings → Export Data.
  • Right to Rectification: Edit or recalibrate any previous check-in entry from the History portal, or update your demographic profile in Settings.
  • Right to Erasure (Right to Be Forgotten): Delete your entire profile, logs, notes, goals, and credentials permanently under Settings → Delete Account. All records are purged immediately.

6. Data Retention Policy

Your telemetry is preserved exclusively for the lifespan of your active account. When you initiate account deletion, all associated data rows cascade and are permanently destroyed from our live datastore.

7. Non-Clinical Wellness Tool Disclaimer

DayTone is an informational wellness self-tracking platform. AI-computed burnout risk classifications are not clinical or psychiatric diagnoses and must never replace licensed medical evaluations. If you are experiencing acute distress, please reach out to qualified healthcare providers or 24/7 crisis helplines.

Questions regarding data protection or rights? Reach out directly.
